# Vouchr — Brand Positioning

**Product name:** Vouchr  
**Tagline:** Pay for results, not reach.

---

## One-paragraph positioning

Vouchr is merchant-owned referral infrastructure for local and regional businesses that rely on word-of-mouth. Instead of coupons, ads, or a marketplace, it lets you issue branded vouchers, turn loyal customers into a measurable, reward-based distribution channel, and pay them in your own store credit when their friends buy. You keep control of the brand and the relationship, and you only pay when a referred customer converts—not for clicks or impressions. Referral, measured and rewarded, for SMBs who want new customers and trust without losing control or budget to low-ROI ads.

---

> When a customer shares your voucher with a friend and that friend buys, the customer earns your store credit. It’s all branded, tracked, and controlled by you—and you only pay when it works.

## What to say / not say

**Never say:** discount platform, deal site, promo tool.  
**Always say:** referral, credit, merchant-owned, pay-for-results.

---

## ICP

- **Primary:** Local & regional merchants (cafes, salons, gyms, rentals, services, retail); SMBs who rely on word-of-mouth; businesses tired of ads with unclear ROI.
- **Secondary:** Franchise-style and multi-location SMBs.
- **Not for:** Influencer marketers, big-brand ad buyers, discount hunters.

---

## Pricing positioning (no numbers)

- Infrastructure, not marketing.
- Cheaper than ads.
- Pay only when value is created.  
**Phrase:** "You pay for customers, not clicks."

---

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "Isn't this just coupons?" | No. Coupons discount. This rewards referrals. |
| "Will people abuse it?" | Credits are locked, controlled, and merchant-specific. |
| "Do I need an app?" | No. It works instantly on any phone. |

## Demo pitch (under 3 minutes)

**Order:** 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6.

1. **Open voucher landing** — Show `/v/[id]`, merchant brand, value, one clear CTA.
2. **Share** — Tap "Share & Earn Credit", get `/r/[id]`, show native share or copy.
3. **Redeem as friend** — Open link in new session, redeem online or in-store (QR).
4. **Confirm** — Staff confirms in dashboard; show redemption moving to "Confirmed".
5. **Credit unlock** — Show referrer's wallet: locked → available after confirm.
6. **Apply credit** — Checkout demo: apply credit, new total. Done.

**Talking point:** "Branded, tracked, controlled by you. You only pay when it works."
